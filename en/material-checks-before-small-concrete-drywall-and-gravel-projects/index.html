Material Checks Before Small Concrete, Drywall, and Gravel Projects

Some home project materials are annoying to under-order. A missing box of flooring is inconvenient, but a shortage of concrete, drywall sheets, or driveway gravel can stop the work completely. These materials are heavy, delivery-sensitive, and sometimes difficult to return.

Before buying, I like to run a quick quantity check in the same format the store uses: bags, sheets, cubic yards, or tons.
